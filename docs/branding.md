# Branding Guidelines
## Fauls House Studio

Version 1.0

---

# Brand Essence

Fauls House Studio is not just a rehearsal space.

It is a creative home.

A place where ideas become music.

A place where musicians grow together.

The digital experience should communicate professionalism without becoming cold, and creativity without becoming chaotic.

Every screen should feel calm, confident, and premium.

---

# Brand Positioning

Professional Music Studio

Modern Booking Experience

Premium Equipment

Creative Community

Trusted Service

Fast & Easy Reservation

---

# Brand Mission

To provide musicians with the easiest, fastest, and most enjoyable studio booking experience while showcasing the quality of the studio through elegant digital interaction.

---

# Brand Vision

Become one of the most trusted and modern music studio brands by combining technology, premium customer experience, and professional rehearsal facilities.

---

# Brand Values

## Simplicity

Everything should be easy to understand.

No unnecessary interface.

No unnecessary interaction.

---

## Professionalism

Every detail reflects quality.

Photography.

Typography.

Spacing.

Motion.

Everything matters.

---

## Creativity

Music is creativity.

The website should inspire creativity without overwhelming users.

---

## Transparency

Prices are clear.

Schedules are clear.

Facilities are clear.

No hidden information.

---

## Trust

Booking should feel safe.

Information should feel reliable.

The interface should reduce uncertainty.

The visual identity combines four inspirations.

Apple

Large spacing.

Elegant typography.

Premium storytelling.

---

Linear

Clean layouts.

Strong hierarchy.

Excellent readability.

Subtle interactions.

---

Industrial feeling.

Creative energy.

Premium craftsmanship.

Modern simplicity.

---

Vindes

Authentic photography.

Youthful creative spirit.

Dark visual language.

# Color Usage Rules

Dark colors dominate.

Accent colors highlight.

Never reverse this hierarchy.

The accent color should never exceed approximately 10% of the screen.

White space is more valuable than colorful interfaces.

---

# Shadow Philosophy

Shadows should be soft.

No harsh floating cards.

No exaggerated blur.

The interface should feel grounded.

---

# Photography Direction

Photography is one of the strongest branding assets.

Use real studio photography.

Avoid stock images whenever possible.

Preferred content:

Studio interior

Drum set

Mixing console

Microphones

Amplifiers

Guitar wall

Recording sessions

Band rehearsals

Warm lighting

---

# Illustration Style

Minimal.

Simple.

Geometric.

Never cartoonish.

Never childish.

---

# Icon Style

Use Lucide Icons.

Outlined icons only.

2px stroke.

Rounded edges.

Consistent size.

Avoid filled icons.

---

# Motion Personality

Motion should feel like music.

Rhythmic.

Smooth.

Balanced.

Elegant.

Not playful.

Not exaggerated.

---

Animation should communicate confidence.

Never excitement.

---

# Sound (Optional Future)

Subtle interface sounds.

Button click.

Booking confirmation.

Studio ambience.

Only if users explicitly enable them.

Never autoplay audio.

---

# Logo Philosophy

The logo should communicate professionalism.

Avoid complex symbols.

Simple typography is preferred.

Possible concepts:

FH monogram

Minimal waveform

Sound wave

Studio doorway

Recording room outline

Abstract acoustic panel

Keep it scalable.

The logo should work in monochrome.

---

# Tone of Voice

Professional.

Friendly.

Clear.

Direct.

Helpful.

Never robotic.

Never overly casual.

Never use unnecessary marketing buzzwords.

---

Good Example

"Choose the studio that matches your rehearsal needs."

Bad Example

"The ultimate revolutionary music experience awaits you."

---

# User Experience Promise

Users should never feel lost.

Users should never feel overwhelmed.

Users should always know the next step.

Every interaction should increase confidence.
